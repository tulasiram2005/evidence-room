# Three concepts
## Ledger Audit — chosen
Warm paper, ink rules and lime highlights.
Editorial serif headlines, restrained monospace figures.
An investigation flows from verdict to evidence and uncertainty.
Receipts express rejected records without exposing customer identities.
Chosen because the trust decisions are central, not decorative dashboard cards.
## Signal Desk
Dark laboratory panels and amber diagnostics.
Dense tabular numbers with a vertical instrument rail.
Compare source and trusted values like calibrated readings.
Strong operational feel, but risks implying data is objectively calibrated.
Not chosen for this management-facing investigation.
## Margin Notes
White manuscript with red editorial annotations.
Narrow prose column beside wide reconciliation graphics.
Footnotes and links foreground traceability.
Calm, readable and print-oriented, but weaker interactive scenario identity.
Not chosen; retain marginal notes as a secondary device.
